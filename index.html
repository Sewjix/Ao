About Studio

We are a collective of digital natives who challenge.

MONO

Born in Tokyo, raised in Saigon — MONO is more than a studio. We are a carefully assembled collective of creatives, strategists, and technologists united by one belief: design has the power to transform businesses and move people.

We operate without hierarchy. Every member of our team is personally involved from brief to execution, ensuring quality runs through every pixel, every word, every interaction.
