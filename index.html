Investment Thesis

Bitcoin is the apex monetary technology.

₿

Founded in 2017 on a single conviction — that Bitcoin represents the first genuinely scarce digital asset and will become the global reserve currency of the 21st century — BASTION operates as a permanent capital vehicle with no redemption pressure and no exit timeline.

We hold Bitcoin in our treasury not as speculation but as a strategic imperative. Simultaneously, we deploy venture capital into the companies building Bitcoin's infrastructure: custody, payments, mining, Layer 2, and sovereignty tooling.
